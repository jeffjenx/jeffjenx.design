01 · Brand Foundation

What Jeff Jenx is

A single-person LLC operating as a unified commercial storefront and creative studio. Products, consulting, and communities organized under one identity.

What it is

A single-person LLC operating as a unified commercial storefront and creative studio. Products, consulting, and communities organized under one identity.

What it is not

Not corporate. Not trendy. Not trying to look bigger than it is. Not sacrificing quality for speed. Not collecting your data.

Origin

"Jeff Jenx" is a compression of Jeffrey Jenkins into 8 characters — the maximum of a 1980s computer login. The name carries that constraint as a feature, not a limitation.

Promise

Everything released under this name is built with care, tested to work, and designed to last. Small output, high standard.

04 · Text Fallback System

Plain-text fallback

Used wherever images are unavailable — plain text browsers, markdown, README files, git history, screen readers, terminal output.

[JJ] Jeff Jenx
Brackets are structural notation, not decoration. They are the square mark expressed in ASCII.
Never replace brackets with parentheses, pipes, or other delimiters. [JJ] is the canonical form.
Category variant: [JJ] Jeff Jenx · GB Home — separator is a centered dot, not a dash.

07 · Design Principles

Principles

Six principles that govern every design decision under this brand.

Progressive

Works with no CSS and no JS. Each layer above that is additive. The brand applies at every layer.

Earned

Every element must justify its presence. If it doesn't communicate, it doesn't ship.

Scalable

Designed at 8px. Comfortable at 16px. Spacious at 64px. One system, every resolution.

Private

Zero personal data collected or stored. Privacy is a design constraint, not a policy checkbox.

Modular

New product lines plug into the system via accent color. The framework never needs to change.

Honest

Sharp corners. No gradients. No shadows. No decoration. The work speaks for itself.
